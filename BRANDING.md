# Omega Technology: Brand Analysis (Stage 1)

Note: values below are visual estimates from the supplied images. Exact hex codes
should be sampled from the original logo files once they are in the project.

## Logo
- Mark: an Omega (Ω) whose inner space holds a "T" (Omega + Technology). Angular,
  chamfered feet and a T with slanted top ends. Reads as engineered and precise.
- Lockup A (primary, dark): mark + "OMEGA" in silver-white gradient + "TECHNOLOGY"
  in cyan, wide letter-spacing, flanked by thin rules.
- Lockup B (light): "OMEGA TECH" + "INNOVATION BUILDS TOMORROW" on white.
- Circular badge variants (with and without filled disc) for avatars/favicons.
- Rule: never redraw, recolor or distort the logo. Use the supplied files only.

## Visual identity
- Dark, cinematic navy with electric blue/cyan neon glow and light flares.
- Glassy rounded cards with glowing blue borders (services poster).
- Thin cyan divider lines under headlines; two-tone headlines (white + cyan).
- Imagery: code on screens, glass offices, city skyline with mountains at dusk.
- Tech motifs: globe with network nodes, servers, database cylinders, phone.

## Typography (proposed)
- Display/headings (Latin): a wide techno sans close to the wordmark (Michroma or Orbitron for accents).
- Body (Latin): Montserrat (matches the banner copy).
- Dari/Persian: Vazirmatn (clean, geometric, variable weights).
